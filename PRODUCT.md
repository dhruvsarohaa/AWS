# Airday

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product purpose

Compare available air evidence with outdoor activity times, choose a practical response, and retain the decision with its original evidence. The current React/Vite application supports local planning without an account.

## Users

Repository-derived audience: people planning walks, school activities, outdoor work, or commutes in India. More specific audience priorities are not confirmed.

## Capabilities and constraints

Today, Plan, Evidence, History, and Settings are persistent views. Local records, favorites, feedback, backups, and offline controls already exist. The active release is R0–R2; shared and external R3 connections are paused, with R4/R5 deferred. Preserve the gates and existing persistence, recovery, and decision workflows.

Regional forecasts and station observations are independent. Missing, retained, delayed, and failed evidence must remain distinguishable. PM2.5 thresholds are user rules, not official AQI or safety declarations. Never invent live readings, provider access, health benefits, or verified delivery.

## Brand commitments

Keep Airday's name and leaf mark. The user requests clean, premium, elegant presentation, smooth interaction, and restrained glass surfaces across the existing application. Existing working stack and features take priority over framework migration. Proceed without approval pauses under the user's deadline instructions.

## Evidence on hand

README.md, FEATURE_IMPLEMENTATION_MAP.md, R0_R2_RELEASE_STATUS.md, and SUBMISSION_UI_AUDIT.md describe current functionality and limitations. Browser checks use labelled synthetic local evidence; live provider and AWS acceptance is separate.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Preserve English/Hindi controls, keyboard focus, native dialogs and form affordances, 44px touch targets, readable contrast, reduced motion, and reduced transparency. Mobile web remains the same product.
